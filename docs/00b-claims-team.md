## The liability-side sequence (Hartford CD guidelines; CLM)

1. Suit tendered; the claim professional confirms coverage and sends the coverage or reservation-of-rights letter.
2. Conflict check with counsel; answer by the next business day.
3. Counsel acknowledges within 1 business day of clearing conflicts; contacts the insured within 3 days.
4. Day 14: initial litigation action plan agreed. Day 30: defense budget.
5. Counsel requests **prior written approval** to retain experts (rate, scope, budget); early retention encouraged.
6. Joint site inspections under an agreed protocol; notice before destructive testing.
7. Day 90: counsel's initial assessment (allegations, facts, damages, options); then status reports at least
   every 90 days, plus "immediately reportable events", including "outbound risk transfer opportunities".
8. Reserve reviews; roundtable for strategy.
9. Pre-mediation report 45 days before mediation; pre-trial 60 days before trial.
10. Counsel requests settlement authority with a valuation and allocation rationale; the handler grants or escalates.

## Disagreements

- Counsel vs carrier on the litigation plan: counsel's "independent professional judgment" (DRI).
- Insurer vs insured on strategy: counsel "cannot get in the middle"; separate coverage counsel (CLM).
- Relying on an independent consultant presumes good faith unless the advice is "patently wrong or a mere pretext"
  (*Montgomery v. Travelers*, Ga. Ct. App. 2021, via [Cozen](https://www.propertyinsurancelawobserver.com/2021/08/31/no-bad-faith-when-insurer-relied-on-opinion-of-independent-consultant/)).
- How a carrier weighs two of its own experts who disagree: UNSOURCED.
